The issue lies in the use of the `initial_output_dir` parameter in the `filedialog.asksaveasfilename()` function in your code. According to the error traceback:

```text
_tkinter.TclError: bad option "-initial_output_dir": must be -confirmoverwrite, -defaultextension, -filetypes, -initialdir, -initialfile, -parent, -title, or -typevariable
```
